1. Client Lifecycle & Workflow

From initial service request to case resolution and transparent timeline tracking.

1

Request a Legal Service

Navigate to Request Service in your client portal. Select a legal service category (Litigation, Corporate, Family, Real Estate, IP), provide a clear summary and description of your legal situation, and upload supporting documents.

2

Live Case Progress Tracking

Once administrative review completes and counsel is assigned, monitor your matter via the 6-stage milestone tracker:

1. Request Submitted → 2. Lawyer Assigned → 3. Documents Under Review → 4. Case Preparation → 5. Court / Legal Proceedings → 6. Case Completed
3

Consultation Scheduling & Secure Communication

Book consultations with your attorney directly with automatic conflict prevention. Use the case message room to send privileged communications directly to your assigned attorney.

2. Attorney Case Management Workflow

Tools for managing assigned caseloads, legal proceedings, filings, and client consultations.

1

Review Assigned Matters

View your active case list on the Attorney Dashboard. Inspect initial client intake statements and filed exhibits.

2

Case Progression & Milestone Updates

Advance case status through the legal lifecycle. Each status change prompts for a legal progress note and updates the client's timeline in real-time.

3

Calendar & Client Consultations

Review incoming appointment requests, approve confirmed dates, or propose alternative times.

3. Firm Administration & Governance

Full operational oversight, user credential controls, billing, and practice analytics.

1

Staff Management & Role Delegation

Manage user roles, activate or suspend credentials, and onboard attorneys with Bar registration details.

2

Service Request Triage & Attorney Assignment

Evaluate new client legal inquiries and convert approved inquiries into formal firm cases, assigning appropriate counsel based on specialization.

3

Invoicing, Reports & Audit Records

Issue invoices to clients, monitor collections, export practice statistics to CSV, and inspect security audit logs.
